Attio MCP: The Official Server and What It Can Do

Attio maintains an official hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) server at mcp.attio.com/mcp.

## What the Attio MCP server does Model Context Protocol (MCP) is the open standard that lets an AI client — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and others — talk to an outside app through a shared interface. Attio maintains an official hosted server at `mcp.attio.com/mcp`, and it authenticates you as your own Attio user, so the AI gets exactly the permissions your account already has: reads are auto-approved, and writes ask for confirmation before they touch your data.
