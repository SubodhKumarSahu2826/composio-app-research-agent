Executive Summary

What this implementation demonstrates

The system is implemented as a deterministic, checkpointed research workflow rather than a single LLM prompt. It retrieves evidence, structures findings, verifies claims, scores research quality, and aggregates the completed sample for decision-oriented comparison.

Evidence-first research

Structured reasoning

Research output is validated into typed application findings covering authentication, access, API, MCP, buildability, and evidence.

Verification + ranking
